A 20-minute action plan

  1. Choose one update that maps to a real weekly task.
  2. Record the current time, cost, and error rate.
  3. Run a small test with a clear success condition.

What not to do

Do not connect sensitive customer data, replace a dependable process, or buy a larger plan before the pilot has a measurable result. New capabilities often look impressive in a demonstration while adding hidden review work in daily use.
